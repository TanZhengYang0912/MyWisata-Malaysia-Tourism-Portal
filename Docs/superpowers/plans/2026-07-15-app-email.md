# App Email Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task with review checkpoints.

**Goal:** Add server-side Gmail SMTP transactional emails for checkout/top-up and withdrawal lifecycle events without changing unrelated modules.

**Architecture:** A server-only Nodemailer adapter renders typed email templates and persists email intents in an `email_outbox` table keyed by a unique business event. Existing Stripe and withdrawal boundaries enqueue events after their current financial/state operations; sending failures are recorded and do not alter those operations.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Nodemailer, Supabase PostgreSQL migration/RLS, Vitest.

## Global Constraints

- Use `smtp.gmail.com` on port `587` with a Gmail App Password; never use or log the normal Gmail password.
- Never expose SMTP secrets to client bundles or `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` variables.
- Do not modify unrelated modules or the pre-existing `app/api/admin/kyc/submissions/route.ts` change.
- Do not include IC/passport contents, KYC paths, tokens, or secrets in email payloads.
- Existing payment, wallet, withdrawal, KYC, and Stripe Connect authorization/state transitions remain unchanged.
