[`jwarby/malaysia-state-flag-icon-css`](https://github.com/jwarby/malaysia-state-flag-icon-css),
specifically its [`flags/1x2`](https://github.com/jwarby/malaysia-state-flag-icon-css/tree/master/flags/1x2)
directory. Line endings are normalized to LF for repository consistency. The
